# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated:** July 24, 2026

## Overview

Langly ("we", "our", "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have in relation to our application.

## Information We Collect

**Langly does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.**

All learning data, including vocabulary, progress, scores, and preferences, is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

## Data Storage

- All app data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework
- No data is uploaded to any server or cloud service
- No analytics or tracking tools are used
- No cookies or similar technologies are employed

## Third-Party Services

Langly does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

### In-App Purchases

In-app purchases are processed through Apple's App Store. Apple's own privacy policy governs the handling of payment information. Langly does not have access to your payment details, Apple ID, or purchase history.

## Children's Privacy

Langly does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age. Since we do not collect any data at all, this policy inherently protects all users regardless of age.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above and published within the app and at our support URL.
