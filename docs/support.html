Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is Langly really 100% offline?

A: Yes! All translations happen on your device. No internet connection required after download.

Q: How many words can I add?

A: You can add up to 150 words across 10 custom blocks in the Vocabulary module.

Q: Can I use Langly to learn other languages?

A: Currently Langly supports English ↔ Brazilian Portuguese. More languages are coming soon.

Q: Is there a limit to how long I can practice?

A: No time limits at all. Practice as long as you want, on whatever you want.

Q: How do I reset my progress?

A: You can reset your progress in the app settings. This will clear all your vocabulary and progress data.
