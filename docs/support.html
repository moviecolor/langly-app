How to Use Langly

1. Getting Started — When you open Langly you'll see 4 modules: Vocabulário (your vocabulary builder, open now) plus Frases Comuns, Pronúncia and Perguntas e Respostas (coming soon). Tap any block of words to open it and start playing.
2. Vocabulary Blocks — Words are organized into blocks you control:
  • Tap Adicionar Bloco de Palavras to create a new block
  • Add up to 150 words across 10 custom blocks
  • Edit any block anytime — tap it and manage the words
  • Everything saves instantly on your device
3. Match & Bet Game — Play Combinar e Apostar to match English and Portuguese pairs before the timer runs out. Use Misturar Todos os Blocos for a bigger challenge. Matching games are the fastest way to make words stick.
4. Audio Mode & Commuting — Turn any block into a looping audio playlist: pick your blocks, choose repeat count and pause intervals, then listen hands-free. Perfect for the subway, bus, or walk to work.
💡 Commuter math: 20 minutes on the train ≈ 15 new words. Run Audio Mode with earbuds in and the words repeat until they stick.
5. Offline & Privacy — Langly is built to work with no connection at all:
  • All translations happen on your device
  • No account, no tracking, no ads
  • Your data (vocabulary, progress, scores) stays locally on your device
  • Reset your progress anytime in Settings

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is Langly really 100% offline?

A: Yes! All translations happen on your device. No internet connection required after download.

Q: How many words can I add?

A: You can add up to 150 words across 10 custom blocks in the Vocabulary module.

Q: What do I get with Langly Premium?

A: Premium unlocks all learning modules (Frases Comuns, Pronúncia, Perguntas e Respostas) as they're released, plus priority support. Vocabulary, the match game, and Audio Mode are free for everyone.

Q: How do I cancel my subscription?

A: Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your iPhone and tap Cancel Subscription. You'll keep access until the end of the current billing period. Cancellations take effect at least 24 hours before the next renewal.

Q: Can I use Langly to learn other languages?

A: Currently Langly supports English ↔ Brazilian Portuguese. More languages are coming soon.

Q: Is there a limit to how long I can practice?

A: No time limits at all. Practice as long as you want, on whatever you want.

Q: How do I reset my progress?

A: You can reset your progress in the app settings. This will clear all your vocabulary and progress data.
